## What Humanity Score evaluates

### Agency
Can people understand, control, override, and reverse what the AI system does?

### Value Distribution
Who benefits? What happens to user data, switching freedom, and incentive alignment?

### Human Connection
Does the product support collaboration and accessibility without silently replacing or degrading important human relationships?
