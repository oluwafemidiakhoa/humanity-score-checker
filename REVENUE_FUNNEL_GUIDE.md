# Humanity Score Revenue Funnel Guide

Use public audit evidence to support qualified business conversations while preserving audit independence.

## Qualification

A prospect is qualified when the product and official domain are verified, public primary-source evidence supports an audit, and a legitimate business contact route exists. Prefer product, trust, governance, partnerships, sales, or founder contacts over customer-support queues.

## Offer timing

Lead with a specific finding or evidence gap and the public audit. Invite factual correction and ask for the appropriate owner. Present the $499 Founder Audit only after the prospect asks about scope, price, deliverables, a meeting, or a commercial next step.

## Independence

Payment cannot influence the score. Public audits are not endorsements or certifications. Missing evidence remains explicit under the current methodology. Do not promise sales, fundraising, compliance, procurement, or performance outcomes.

## Funnel definitions

Qualified outreach means an evidence-backed audit, verified identity, and legitimate commercial route. A meaningful reply addresses the audit, routing, correction, meeting, scope, or offer. Purchase intent means the prospect asks about price, deliverables, payment, contracting, timing, or how to proceed. A completed payment is a confirmed successful live Stripe Founder Audit payment.

Track one canonical record per prospect. Resends are not new qualified outreaches. Anonymous or expired checkout sessions without prospect identity are not proof of buyer intent or checkout friction.
