# Governance Crosswalk

Humanity Score is **not** a compliance certification. This crosswalk is a decision-support mapping showing where Humanity Score evidence may help a buyer or governance team ask better questions.

It does not establish conformance with NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, the EU AI Act, or any other legal or regulatory framework.

| Humanity Score area | Example governance concern | Potentially relevant framework themes |
|---|---|---|
| User control | Ability to disable, constrain, or require approval | NIST AI RMF Govern/Manage; ISO/IEC 42001 operational controls |
| Reversibility | Rollback, deletion, export, recovery | NIST Manage; lifecycle controls; data governance |
| Transparency | Logs, explanations, provenance | NIST Map/Measure; transparency/documentation obligations |
| Human override | Human approval and escalation | Human oversight / accountability themes |
| User benefit | Measurable customer benefit | Impact assessment / stakeholder benefit |
| Data rights | Retention, deletion, training use, portability | Data governance, privacy, information management |
| Lock-in | Exit paths and interoperability | Third-party/vendor risk; operational resilience |
| Incentive alignment | Business incentives vs. user interests | Governance, accountability, risk incentives |
| Collaboration | Human review and shared work | Human factors and organizational controls |
| Substitution risk | Boundaries on automation | Human oversight and role allocation |
| Social wellbeing | Workload, dependence, trust | Impact monitoring / societal and human factors |
| Accessibility | Disability, language, affordability, usability | Accessibility, inclusion, affected-person considerations |

## Use in procurement

Humanity Score can contribute evidence to:

- AI vendor due-diligence questionnaires
- procurement review packets
- internal AI risk committee discussions
- vendor reassessment after material product changes
- evidence requests for missing documentation

The output should be treated as one evidence layer, not as a substitute for legal review, security testing, model validation, privacy assessment, or formal compliance processes.
