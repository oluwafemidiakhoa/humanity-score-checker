# October 11, 2026 — Letta and Laminar public-source research

**Status: Research only / not a Humanity Score audit receipt.** No numerical scores, badges, independent source snapshot signatures, or human claim-review signatures are asserted. Published company documentation is evidence of what is documented, not proof of outcomes in every deployment.

## Letta (letta.com)

- **Human oversight:** [Permissions](https://docs.letta.com/configuration/permissions) documents standard, strict, acceptEdits and unrestricted modes. Its interactive CLI defaults to unrestricted; users seeking approval gates must select a stricter mode.
- **Inspectable user state:** [Letta app](https://docs.letta.com/platform/desktop-app) documents viewing and editing agent memory, skills and channels, and checking scheduled-run history.
- **Collaboration controls:** [Team permissions](https://docs.letta.com/teams/permissions) documents private-by-default agents/conversations, admin/editor/analyst roles, and separately enforced local tool permissions.
- **Portability and accessibility:** [Docs](https://docs.letta.com/) describes open-source self-hosting and selectable LLM backends; [pricing](https://docs.letta.com/pricing) lists free BYOK entry, $20 monthly Pro, $20/month API plan with metered execution/active-agent costs, and $20/seat/month Teams Pro. Those published prices refer to *Letta*, not Humanity Score.
- **Data-rights qualification:** [Privacy policy](https://www.letta.com/privacy-policy/) says hosted-service request/response data is collected and may be used for service improvement including future model training; it distinguishes hosted use from running one's own backends where prompts are not collected. Do not describe Letta as having a blanket no-training guarantee.
- **Commercial review route:** [Letta's business contact form](https://www.letta.com/contact/) asks for work email, company and business use case. Do not use its Discord support/community channel for sales outreach.

**Unresolved:** Real-world accessibility and human social wellbeing outcomes; provider-specific training commitments for all hosted configurations; independent attestations of feature effectiveness.

## Laminar / LMNR AI, Inc. (laminar.sh)

- **Trace transparency:** [Product page](https://laminar.sh/) documents trace transcripts of agent LLM/tool calls, token/cost visibility, failure clustering, evaluations, annotation interfaces and alerting.
- **Deployment choice:** [Product page](https://laminar.sh/) describes Apache-2.0 open source and self-hosting. [Privacy notice](https://laminar.sh/policies/privacy) says self-hosted data is not transferred to Laminar for training.
- **Commercial transparency:** [Pricing](https://laminar.sh/pricing) publishes Free, $30/month Starter, $150/month Pro and custom Enterprise, including retention, limits and usage overages; do not infer future price guarantees.
- **Data-rights qualification — essential:** [Data Use](https://laminar.sh/policies/data-use) and [Terms](https://laminar.sh/policies/terms) state Signal Run Data may be used to train Laminar's models when Privacy Mode is off. It defaults **off for Free/Starter**, **on for Pro+**, and locked on for signed-DPA workspaces. Turning it back on stops future training use but cannot undo already trained models; scope starts with Signal runs generated on or after September 10, 2026. This must not be represented as blanket no-training.
- **Privacy governance:** [Privacy notice](https://laminar.sh/policies/privacy) differentiates account/controller data from customer traces/processor data, describes end-user privacy requests, redaction limits and retention.
- **Commercial review route:** **founders@lmnr.ai** is printed in the footer of [Laminar's main site](https://laminar.sh/) and [pricing](https://laminar.sh/pricing), making it a verified public founder/business route (not support).

**Unresolved:** Demonstrable outcomes for end users, guaranteed human approvals in downstream agents, model-training exposure of a particular customer's prior Signal runs, and independent evidence-review attestation.
