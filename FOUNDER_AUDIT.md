# Humanity Score Founder Audit

**Price:** $499 USD, one-time  
**Checkout:** https://book.stripe.com/eVq3cu0N71pucyIf8Eao80b

The Founder Audit is a deeper, evidence-backed review of an AI product using the Humanity Score methodology.

## What the buyer receives

- Evidence-backed Humanity Score assessment
- Agency, Value Distribution, and Human Connection dimension scores
- Criterion-level evidence review
- Evidence gaps and limitations
- Prioritized action brief
- Buyer due-diligence questions
- Shareable verification receipt with deterministic report hash
- Explicit UNKNOWN criteria when evidence is missing
- Duplicate-claim and contradiction checks
- Source-integrity metadata, including snapshot hashes when independently retrieved
- Clear distinction between documented evidence, missing evidence, and interpretation

## What checkout collects

The live Stripe checkout collects:

- Company or organization
- AI product URL
- Main audit goal or concern
- Payment and customer receipt information

## How fulfillment works

1. Buyer completes the $499 checkout.
2. Humanity Score reviews the submitted product URL and public evidence.
3. Public evidence can be independently snapshotted and hashed for provenance.
4. Evidence is de-duplicated, contradiction-checked, and scored against the published rubric.
5. Missing criteria remain UNKNOWN rather than receiving a neutral score.
6. A decision brief is produced from the evidence-backed audit.
7. The buyer receives the completed audit artifact and verification receipt.

If the available evidence is insufficient, the audit must report the evidence gaps rather than inventing findings or awarding a favorable score.

## What payment does not buy

Payment does **not** buy:

- a higher Humanity Score
- a GREEN badge
- regulatory approval
- legal or compliance certification
- safety certification
- removal of documented negative evidence

Scores change only when the underlying evidence or methodology changes.

## Scope boundary

The $499 Founder Audit is designed for one AI product and a public-source evidence review. Private evidence, portfolio reviews, procurement engagements, ongoing monitoring, or enterprise controls require a separately scoped engagement.

The current product is an independent AI-product due-diligence layer, not a replacement for enterprise AI inventory, approval workflow, runtime guardrails, or formal compliance systems.
